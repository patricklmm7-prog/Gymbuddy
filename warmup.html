Warm Up Properly

A proper warm-up increases blood flow, raises body temperature, and prepares your joints and muscles for training. It can reduce injury risk significantly.

Recommended Routine (5–10 minutes)

  • 2–3 minutes light cardio (bike, row, jump rope, or brisk walk)
  • Dynamic stretches: arm circles, leg swings, hip openers, torso twists
  • 1–2 light sets of your first exercise

Tip: Never skip the warm-up, especially before heavy lifting. A good warm-up can reduce injury risk by up to 50%.
