Stay Hydrated

Hydration Guidelines

  • Before: Drink 500ml water 2 hours before training
  • During: Sip 150–250ml every 15–20 minutes
  • After: Drink 1.5× the fluid you lost (weigh yourself before and after)

For sessions longer than 60 minutes, consider adding electrolytes.

Note: Even 2% dehydration can reduce strength and endurance performance.
