Rest & Recovery

Recovery Essentials

  • Sleep 7–9 hours (growth hormone peaks at night)
  • Rest at least 48 hours between training the same muscle group
  • Protein intake: 1.6–2.2g per kg of body weight
  • Light activity (walking or stretching) on rest days helps recovery
  • Consider a deload week every 6–8 weeks

Muscles grow during rest, not just during training.
